Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information M&P Developers ("we", "us") collects through this website (mp-developers-zm.github.io) and the M&P Lock service, why we use it, and the choices you have. We are based in Lusaka, Zambia, and we follow the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 of Zambia.

In short: the service catalog does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not send what you type to us. You only share information with us when you choose to message us on WhatsApp, or when a shop uses M&P Lock.

1. The service catalog (this website)

  • Your enquiry list. The services you add to your enquiry are saved in your own browser (local storage, key mp-enquiry) so the list is still there when you come back. It never leaves your device unless you send it. You can clear it with the Clear list button in the enquiry window, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • Your name in the enquiry. If you type your name or business, it is only placed into the WhatsApp message. We receive it only if you send that message.
  • Search. What you type in the search box stays in your browser.
  • Fonts and scripts. Our pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, and the M&P Lock dashboard loads two open-source code libraries from the jsDelivr network. When your browser fetches these files, Google and jsDelivr receive your IP address and browser details, as any web server does. They set no cookies for us and we receive no information from them.
  • Hosting. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical information such as your IP address and browser type when your browser loads the pages, and may keep it in its server logs for security. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

2. When you contact us on WhatsApp

When you message us on WhatsApp, we receive your phone number, your WhatsApp name and whatever you send us. We use this only to reply to you, prepare quotes and deliver the work you ask for. WhatsApp is run by WhatsApp LLC (Meta) under its own terms and privacy policy.

3. M&P Lock (for shops that sell phones on payment plans)

M&P Lock helps a shop keep track of phones sold on lay-by and lock a phone remotely if payments are late, as agreed between the shop and its customer.

  • Shop owner account. The owner's email address and password (stored securely, never in readable form) are used to log in.
  • Records the shop keeps. Customer name, customer phone number, phone model, IMEI (optional), price, deposit, payment plan, payments received, and whether the phone is active, locked or released.
  • The customer's phone. The M&P Lock app on the phone checks in with our server using the phone's own ID and secret key. We record the time of the last check-in, and the server replies whether the phone should be locked or unlocked.
  • Who decides. The shop decides whose details to enter and when to lock or release a phone, and is responsible to its customers for doing so fairly and lawfully. We store and process these records on the shop's behalf, only to run the service.
  • Where it is stored. M&P Lock data is stored with Supabase in its Frankfurt, Germany (EU) data centre. Only the shop owner's account can see the shop's records.
  • Customers who want to see, correct or delete their details should contact the shop first. You can also contact us using the details below.

4. How we use information

  • To answer enquiries and provide the services you ask for.
  • To run, secure and fix M&P Lock.
  • To keep records the law requires, and to protect our rights if there is a dispute.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Who we share it with

Only the service providers named above (GitHub for hosting, Google Fonts and jsDelivr for page files, Supabase for M&P Lock, and WhatsApp when you message us), and authorities when the law requires it. Some of these providers store data outside Zambia.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and M&P Lock records only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires. A shop can delete a phone's record at any time from its dashboard.

7. Security

We protect data with access controls, encrypted connections and regular security checks. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise that information will never be accessed without permission. If a breach affects your data, we will act as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Under Zambia's Data Protection Act you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to how it is used, and receive a copy of it. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner. To make a request, contact us below. We may need to confirm who you are first.

9. Children

This website and M&P Lock are not meant for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
